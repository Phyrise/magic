# Magic Survival · Spells & fusions

English companion for Magic Survival. Pick the spell the game offers you and see:

- its 3 **attributes** (the choice at max level), with tiers;
- the **fusions** each attribute unlocks, and the partner spell + attribute they need;
- the **ultimate** behind each fusion;
- the fusions where the spell is only an **ingredient** (it gets consumed).

There are two tier lists, **Standard** and **Deus Ex Machina**, plus tier lists for attributes, ultimates and special passives.

The app is a single `index.html` (HTML, CSS, JavaScript and data) with no build step or dependencies.

- Ultimate descriptions are translated from the Japanese wiki.

Tiers are one player's opinion and are tuned for long survival runs. Unofficial fan tool.
